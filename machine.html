The part nobody else does

Generating video is the easy half and everyone sells it. The half that actually costs you money is what happens when the automation gets it wrong. Each of these checks exists because it went wrong on our own shop first:

  • Live price verification. Every video is checked against your listing's current price before it ships. Built after our own prices drifted across three different versions at once and shipped wrong.
  • Frozen-frame detection. A render that stalled is caught before it posts, not after it is public.
  • No two videos share a caption. We measured it: reels of one product sharing a caption took zero views against about a hundred for a unique one. The queue now refuses the duplicate.
  • One video, one platform. Reposting a file across accounts is what earns an unoriginal-content strike. It is impossible here, in code, not by policy.
  • Published means published. A platform once reported seventeen of our videos live when not one of them could be reached by the public. Nothing is marked posted until the link is fetched signed-out.
