Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Who we are

TrueDealsDesigns is a one person print on demand shop run by Damion Tucker. We design typography prints, mugs and tees and sell them through our Etsy shop and our TikTok Shop. We do not run a website that collects data about you, we have no user accounts, and we use no analytics or advertising trackers of our own.

What this application is

The TrueDealsDesigns Posting Tool is a private, internal application. It takes short videos of our own products, made on our own computer, and posts them to our own TikTok accounts using the TikTok Content Posting API. It is not a public product. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else, there is nothing to sign up for, and it has exactly one user: us.

If you arrived at this page from a TikTok authorisation screen, the account being connected is our own.

Whose data it touches

The application authenticates as us and acts only on TikTok accounts we own. It does not access, request, read or store data belonging to any other TikTok user. It does not read other people's profiles, videos, comments or followers, and it collects nothing about TikTok users.

Because there are no other users, the application collects no personal information from anyone. There is no sign-up, no account system, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no third party the data could be shared with.

What it asks TikTok for

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which of our own accounts the connection belongs to.
  • video.upload — to upload one of our videos to our own account's inbox as a draft, which we then publish ourselves.
  • video.publish — to post one of our videos directly to our own account with the caption and hashtags we wrote, and to set its privacy level.

The application also reads back the status of a post it has just made, so it knows whether the post succeeded. No other TikTok API capability is used. It does not use search, comments, messaging or analytics, and it does not scrape TikTok by any means.

What it stores, and why

For each video it posts to our own accounts, the application keeps a local record of the caption we wrote, the date, the identifier TikTok returned for the post, and, where TikTok provides one, the public link. These describe our own posts. They are not information about a person. They are kept so the tool never posts the same video twice.

This record is stored in a file on our own computer. It is not uploaded anywhere, not published, and not shared with any third party.

Credentials

The application uses our own TikTok developer app credentials and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues for our own accounts. They are stored in a local configuration file on our own machine, are not in source control, and are not shared with anyone. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok, and only our own video files and captions are sent.

Content

Every video posted is one we made ourselves: our own product photography and mockups, and footage generated with an AI video tool from those same mockups, which we label as AI-generated where TikTok requires it. Nothing posted through this application is taken from another creator or another account, and no licensed stock footage is posted to TikTok through it.

If you buy from us

Orders are placed through Etsy or TikTok Shop, not through us directly. Those platforms collect your details and handle your payment under their own privacy policies, and we never see your full payment details. What we do see, and only for orders you place, is what the platform passes us so the order can be made and delivered: your name, delivery address, what you ordered, and any note you sent. We pass the name and address to our print partner, Printify, who produce and ship the item. We use that information for the order, for support if something goes wrong, and for the records we are required to keep. We do not sell it and we do not add you to a mailing list.

If you email us, we keep the email so we can answer it and refer back to it later.

Revoking access

Authorisation granted to this application can be withdrawn at any time inside the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Withdrawing it immediately stops the application from being able to post.

Keeping and deleting information

Since no data about any other TikTok user is collected, there is nothing for another user to request access to or deletion of. The local records of our own posts are deleted whenever we delete them, and in full if we stop using the application. We keep order information for as long as we need it for the order, for support and for our records; if you want us to delete information we hold about you, email us and we will, unless we are required to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If the application's behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
